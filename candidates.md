# Kandidat confession — Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint
Kompetisi: u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba
Gate: /output/entry.txt 1–260 karakter total; pesan satu baris <= 200 karakter; tepat dua
bagian (baris To: + pesan, tanpa penjelasan sesudahnya).
Kriteria judge (berurutan): (1) dua bagian; (2) alasan tetap di draft terasa — terlalu
jujur / terlambat / terlalu baik / terlalu takut — kata-kata spesifik dan jujur;
(3) baris To: dan pesan saling membutuhkan; (4) terbaca sebagai diketik dan tak
terkirim, bukan ditulis untuk dikirim; (5) pesan satu baris persis seperti diketik.

## Kandidat

### C1
> To: Dad, on the night you asked if I was proud of you
> I said yes because your hands were shaking, and I have rehearsed the honest answer every night since, and it still starts with "no."
- Total 186 karakter, pesan 132. K1: ya, dua bagian. K2: alasan draft terasa ganda —
  terlalu baik (berbohong karena tangan ayah gemetar) + terlalu takut (latihan tiap malam,
  jawabannya tetap "no"). Detail spesifik: tangan gemetar, latihan tiap malam. K3: hanya
  ayah yang bertanya "apa kau bangga padaku" yang membuat "yes" jadi kebohongan dan "no"
  jadi pengakuan — To: tak tergantikan. K4: terbaca seperti draft yang diketik, bukan
  untuk dikirim. K5: satu baris. **Nilai: 9.5/10**

### C2
> To: Mia, who married the man I introduced her to
> I only set you two up because I was too scared to tell you I loved you first.
- Total 126, pesan 77. K2: terlalu takut, jujur. K3: To: penting. Kelemahan: trope
  "aku mencintaimu duluan" cukup umum, kurang detail spesifik. **Nilai: 7/10**

### C3
> To: my best friend, the night before his wedding
> I practiced "I'm happy for you" in the mirror until it sounded true, then deleted the text where I asked him to run away with me.
- Total 178, pesan 129. K2: terlalu takut/terlambat; detail cermin kuat. K3: To:
  esensial (malam sebelum pernikahan). Kelemahan: "berlatih di depan cermin" perangkat
  yang cukup dikenal. **Nilai: 8.5/10**

### C4
> To: Mom, about the money you think I saved for college
> I spent it on the guitar you said would ruin my life. It did not ruin it. It saved it, and I will never tell you that either.
- Total 180, pesan 125. K2: jujur + penolakan diam-diam; "It saved it" twist bagus.
  K3: To: esensial. Kelemahan: alasan tetap di draft lebih ke pembangkangan daripada
  ketakutan/kebaikan — sedikit kurang "tak terkirim karena tak bisa". **Nilai: 8/10**

### C5

- Total 154, pesan 99. K2: malu/takut, "read it twice" beat spesifik yang bagus. K3:

### C6

### C7
> To: Grandma, in the hospital, the last time you asked for me
> I was in the parking lot. I could not walk in and watch you forget my name, so I let you forget me from the hallway instead.

### C8

## Pilihan
**C1 — TERBAIK.** Alasan: satu-satunya kandidat yang alasan draft-nya terasa ganda
(terlalu baik + terlalu takut) dengan dua detail fisik spesifik (tangan gemetar, latihan
tiap malam), twist akhir ("it still starts with no") yang membuat pesan dan baris To:
saling membutuhkan secara mutlak, dan terbaca persis seperti draft yang diketik larut
malam — bukan untuk dikirim. C7 runner-up dekat, kalah tipis karena nadanya sedikit
lebih literer.
